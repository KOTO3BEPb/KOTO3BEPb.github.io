Privacy Policy for LinkLess

Effective Date: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the LinkLess mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application") created by KOTO3BEPb ("we", "us", or "our").

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not require any user registration or user accounts to use LinkLess. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data, contacts, messages, or text entered into the Application. All input formatting (such as cleaning phone numbers or processing local secret phrases) is performed locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The Application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for advertising and analytics purposes.

Specifically, LinkLess integrates Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect certain device parameters, such as your Advertising ID (IDFA/GAID), IP address, and technical diagnostic data to deliver personalized or non-personalized banner advertisements.

For more details on how Google handles your data, please review Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Data Retention and Security

Since we do not collect personal data or operate remote servers holding user profiles, we do not store your communication history or links. Any secret phrases or notes created within the Application exist only in accordance with your local session or auto-expiration settings.

4. Children's Privacy

LinkLess does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at py3bepb@email.com.
